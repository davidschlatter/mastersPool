Pool Rules & Tiebreakers
  • Choose 6 golfers — one from each tier.
  • At least 4 of your 6 must make the cut.
  • Combined score of your top 4 golfers is how participants are ranked.
  • Lowest combined score wins.

Tiebreakers (in order)

  1. Having the Masters winner picked within your top 4.
  2. Best score of your 5th golfer, then 6th golfer. Golfers that miss the cut are scored +99 after Friday.
  3. Closest guess of the winner's winning score.
  4. Most players to make the cut (max 6).
